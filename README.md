SOLMAR — an open-world crime game in three.js. A sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car: traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a five-star wanted level with police chases, a five-mission story from courier to the big score, pistol combat, a HUD with a live minimap, a title screen and saves, and synthesized sound. Everything original — own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound, nothing from Rockstar Games.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move and drive, mouse to look, E to steal or exit a car, click to shoot, R to reload, P to pause.
